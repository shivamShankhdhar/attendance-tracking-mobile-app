## How to Link in Google Play Console

1. Upload `bizora_how_it_works_with_voiceover_1080p.mp4` to your YouTube channel as **Public** or **Unlisted**.
2. Copy your YouTube video URL (e.g. `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...` or `https://youtu.be/...`).
3. In **Google Play Console**:
   * Navigate to **Store presence** $\rightarrow$ **Main store listing**.
   * Scroll down to **Graphics** $\rightarrow$ **YouTube video**.
   * Paste the video link and click **Save**.
4. The video with crystal-clear voiceover narration will appear prominently at the top of your Google Play Store listing with a native play button overlaying your feature graphic.
